# Controller Playground

A small shared-screen tool for testing game controls. The host puts pairing and live inputs first; the phone puts the active controls within reach. Use quiet, native-feeling typography and warm neutral surfaces, with restrained blue for connection and active input.

Give the wheel and joystick a clear physical form without turning the interface into an illustration. Keep labels direct, controls comfortable, and the layout usable in portrait and landscape. Motion needs a touch alternative and a visible recenter action.
